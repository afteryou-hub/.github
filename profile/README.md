# **AfterYou**

**AfterYou** is a **Digital Vault** platform that protects and manages financial assets and important documents, ensuring privacy, security, and complete control over each user’s digital legacy. Our mission is to combine cutting-edge technology with simplicity and trustworthiness.

---

## **Our Mission**

To safeguard what matters most to each individual by providing secure and intuitive digital solutions. At **AfterYou**, every asset, document, and directive is stored confidentially and accessible only to authorized members of the user’s trusted circle.

---

## **What We Do**

**Asset Protection**  
Securely store bank accounts, investments, digital wallets, and other financial assets with full privacy.

**Digital Legacy Management**  
Allow users to set emergency directives so their trusted circle knows exactly how to handle assets in critical situations.

**Secure Document Storage**  
Upload, organize, and protect important files such as PDFs, images, and statements with end-to-end encryption.

**Trust and Privacy**  
All data is encrypted and distributed across secure environments, ensuring that only authorized individuals have access.

---

## **Our Vision**

To become a leading reference in **digital vaults**, providing advanced technology with an intuitive experience, fostering confidence and security for every user.

---

## **Our Values**

- **Security** – Advanced encryption and protocols guarantee data confidentiality and integrity.  
- **Transparency** – Users maintain full control over who can access their information.  
- **Simplicity** – Clear and intuitive interfaces make managing assets practical and efficient.  
- **Legacy** – Ensure that essential assets and information are protected and accessible when needed.
